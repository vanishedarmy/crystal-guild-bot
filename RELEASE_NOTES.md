# Crystal Guild v1.9.0 Alpha 2 — Economy Balance

Alpha 2 audits the full Gil and Gold Saucer economy with one firm goal: **close actual exploits without turning ordinary progression into a grind.**

## Changes
- Mineral Survey Retainer fee: **500 → 850 Gil**; focused fee **625 → 1,050 Gil**.
- Specialist Acquisition Retainer fee: **1,500 → 1,850 Gil**; focused fee **1,875 → 2,300 Gil**.
- Workshop Press Adamantite fee: **750 → 1,600 Gil**.
- Trading Post construction now requires **15 Ventures** instead of 15 Expeditions, keeping the Level-1 building accessible before the Level-6 Expedition Board.
- Monster Toss payout tiers are now **10 / 15 / 30 / 50 / 80 / 120 MGP** at **75 / 100 / 250 / 400 / 600 / 900** points.

## Intentionally unchanged
Normal daily Gil, monster/Hunt/Raid reward curves, ordinary shop prices, NPC resale ratios, Forge progression, Trading Post fees/tax, Mini Cactpot, Crystal Slots, Chocobo Racing, Triple Triad, daily MGP credits, and Gold Saucer Prize Counter costs all remain unchanged.

## Compatibility
- Runtime/frontend: `1.9.0-economy-balance-alpha2`
- Local schema: **19**
- Hosted sync: **1**
- Portal Action protocol: **36**
- Player reset: **not required**
