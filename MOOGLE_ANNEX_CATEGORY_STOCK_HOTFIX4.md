# Crystal Guild v1.9.0 Alpha 6 — Moogle Annex Category Stock Hotfix 4

- Annex bonus stock is no longer drawn from one generic random pool.
- Fixed bonus composition: **+2 Weapons, +4 Armor/Accessories, +6 Items/Consumables**.
- The original 9-slot rotation remains unchanged: 3 Weapons, 2 Armor, 1 Accessory, 3 Consumables.
- With sufficient unique eligible stock, a completed Annex therefore presents **5 Weapons, 7 Armor/Accessories, and 9 Items = 21 total**.
- If a low-level/job pool cannot supply the full category quota, remaining capacity is filled from other eligible unique shop stock rather than duplicating entries.
- Existing 15-slot Annex caches invalidate automatically because the authoritative target capacity is now 21.
