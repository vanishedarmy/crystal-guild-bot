# Gold Saucer MGP Rebalance Audit — v1.7.1 Alpha 2

**Result: PASS — materially more rewarding, still daily-bounded.**

## Daily credits
- Welcome purse: **500 MGP** unchanged.
- Daily recovery allowance after the welcome day: **125 MGP**.
- Separate daily reward: **50 MGP** once per UTC day, including the welcome day.
- Both daily credits are exactly-once through the existing Gold Saucer history ledger.

## Attractions
- Mini Cactpot: **10 MGP** entry unchanged; sum-6 jackpot **1,250 MGP**. Random-line expected payout is **39.17 MGP**.
- Crystal Slots: **15 MGP** entry unchanged; pair payout **23 MGP**; exact theoretical RTP **97.145%**. Slots remain the repeatable soft sink.
- Monster Toss: **20 MGP** entry unchanged. Payout tiers are **8 / 18 / 35 / 60 / 90 MGP**. The strongest fixed target/style profile is about **129.94% return**, bounded to three games per UTC day.
- Chocobo Racing: entries remain **25 / 35 / 50 MGP**. Podiums are **120/70/35**, **190/105/50**, and **300/160/75 MGP**. Three races per UTC day continue to cap earning throughput.
- Triple Triad: entries remain **20 / 30 / 45 MGP**; draws still refund exact entry. Wins pay **100 / 150 / 240 MGP**. Five matches per UTC day cap skilled-player upside.

## Prize Counter
Prices rise with the richer earning curve: Saucer Regular **500**, Cactpot Connoisseur **1,750**, Toss Tactician **2,500**, Feathered Finisher **3,000**, Jackpot Chaser **3,500**, Triad Tactician **4,000**, Pot Shot Pro **4,500**, Circuit Champion **7,500**, and Card Hall Master **9,000 MGP**.
