# Crystal Guild v1.9.92 — Job Ascension: Job Ability Reference

Each of the 18 Jobs advances independently to Job Level 99. Existing Job Mastery and cross-Job slots still unlock at **JLv.50**; mastering a Job stays a prerequisite for its Ultimate Relic quest. No existing Job ability IDs or passive effect IDs were removed.

**Active ability progression:** JLv.52, 58, 64, 70, 76, 82, 88, 94, 99 (9 new combat actions per Job). **Passive progression:** JLv.55, 65, 75, 85, 95 (5 new passives per Job). Black Mage and White Mage also gain seven missing pre-50 spell unlocks.

The existing White Mage **Cure II** keeps its internal ID and command alias and is now shown as **Cura**, following the traditional Cure → Cura → Curaga → Curaja progression.

Use `!ability <spell name> [target]` during supported active encounters or the Portal's battle controls. Spells use the current resource, target, status and healing rules. Long-range/instant-kill, Reraise-on-death and enemy-buff-dispel mechanics were not invented for this release; those need separate combat-system work.
